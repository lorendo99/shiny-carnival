---
name: Stripe marketplace transfer model
description: Financial and operational implications of delayed Connect transfers for FixMate marketplace repairs.
---

For FixMate's separate-charge-and-transfer model, the platform receives the customer charge and sends the engineer's share to their connected Stripe balance only after customer completion confirmation. Do not describe this as escrow. Stripe processing fees, refunds, disputes, and negative-balance exposure remain platform responsibilities; bank arrival follows the engineer's Stripe payout schedule.

**Why:** The delayed transfer timing does not change the platform's responsibility for the customer charge, and a Stripe balance transfer is distinct from a bank payout.

**How to apply:** Preserve these distinctions in payment copy and future refund, dispute, and payout changes. Verify Connect capability and payout readiness before accepting marketplace payments.
