---
name: Labour marketplace direction
description: Product direction for FixMate's customer-created labour jobs and worker workflow
---

FixMate's marketplace is a local labour-work platform: customers post a job, local workers discover it and send an offer, the customer chooses and pays securely, the worker completes the job, and both sides can track progress and communicate privately in FixMate.

**Why:** The marketplace should compete on a clear end-to-end job flow, not on diagnosis or repairer quote language. Existing repair jobs remain supported as a category and diagnosis handoff, but the primary product framing is broader household work.

**How to apply:** Use job, worker, offer, award, progress, and completion language in marketplace UI. Keep the existing privacy, safety, payment, verification, and timeline rules unchanged.
