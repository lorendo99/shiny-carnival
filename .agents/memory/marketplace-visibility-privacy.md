---
name: Marketplace visibility and privacy
description: Product-approved boundaries for engineer competition, job evidence, and private conversations.
---

Open jobs show only a postcode area; redact full codes from open-job text and prevent sharing them in pre-agreement messages or offers. Public responses omit customer identity, photos, quotes, and private messages. Active engineers may see job photos and competing quote details. Only after both sides agree does the job reserve and the winning engineer receive the full postcode; messages remain private per engineer.

**Why:** The user requires multiple offers and mutual price agreement before reservation, with the full postcode disclosed only to the winning engineer after agreement.

**How to apply:** Preserve these boundaries in marketplace queries, notifications, privacy copy, analytics, exports, and any future realtime or mobile implementation. Customers retain their own full postcode throughout.
