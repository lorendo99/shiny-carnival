---
name: Premium repair saving
description: Product decision for how the FixMate premium marketplace saving is communicated and priced.
---

Present the premium repair benefit as an estimated 15% saving. The engineer submits the final discounted quote; FixMate must not silently or automatically subtract 15% from that quote or the Stripe total.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose an estimated saving rather than an automatic platform-applied discount, so the engineer remains responsible for the final price.

**How to apply:** Keep mobile and web copy non-guaranteed, show the estimate to premium customers, and preserve engineer-submitted totals through quote creation, acceptance, and payment.
