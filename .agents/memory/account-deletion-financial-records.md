---
name: Account deletion and financial records
description: How FixMate balances permanent account deletion with marketplace record-retention obligations.
---

Delete the user's account identity, diagnoses, customer-owned jobs, private messages, local history, and removable evidence. Marketplace records that must remain for legal, fraud, tax, dispute, or payment obligations should be retained only in de-identified form.

**Why:** Apple requires in-app account deletion, while real-world service payments can create legal and financial retention duties that make indiscriminate deletion unsafe.

**How to apply:** Any new account-linked content must be assigned either to the deletion set or to a documented, minimal retention set. Retained records must not keep unnecessary profile or message content.
