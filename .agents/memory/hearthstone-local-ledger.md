---
name: Hearthstone local ledger scope
description: Why the camera-first Hearthstone app is a local utility rather than a bank-connected finance app.
---

Keep Hearthstone Ledger's opening balance and example goal explicitly illustrative unless the user supplies real starting figures or asks to connect a financial source. Manual movements and receipt images belong to this device in the initial version; do not imply bank sync, OCR, or cross-device backup.

**Why:** The approved canvas design used simulated financial figures and a simulated scan. The build request was to turn that selected design into a working product, not to authorize bank access or claim those examples are live money.

**How to apply:** When expanding the ledger, preserve the distinction between example values and user-entered values, and ask for an appropriate data source before presenting actual account balances.
